GOOD QUESTIONS

How it actually works.

Do I get the cook exclusively?

No. The idea is to share a cook across a small cluster of nearby households so each home pays less while the cook gets steadier work.

What happens when my cook is away?

The platform checks available backup capacity first. If a replacement is not possible, the platform can handle the defined service-credit process.

Who buys the groceries?

That depends on the plan and the current implementation. The product keeps grocery spending separate from the cooking service so the costs stay transparent.

Can I choose my cooking time?

You choose a preferred window. The platform builds a workable schedule around the cook's capacity and the other households in the cluster.
