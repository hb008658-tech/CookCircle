Good to know.

Who buys the ingredients?

Your household does. Your cook prepares the meal in your kitchen with what you provide.

How long is a session?

Each session is two hours. Choose a day and an available time when you book.

What if my cook can’t make it?

Let us know and a backup cook can be assigned to your booking.

How do I know the right person arrives?

Each booking comes with a check-in code, so you can confirm the cook assigned to you.

Is my society covered?

Cooks are approved society by society. If yours isn’t listed yet, register it and we’ll be in touch.

Still need a hand?

Send us a message and we’ll get back to you. If your society isn’t on CookCircle yet, you can register it too.
