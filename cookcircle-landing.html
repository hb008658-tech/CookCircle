COMMON QUESTIONS

Start with what you need.

Getting started

How does the shared-cook idea work?+

Households in the same community can be grouped around a compatible cook. The platform helps coordinate requests and the cooking routine.

Households

How do I request a cook?+

Choose an available date and session, tell us the meal and household details, and follow the booking flow shown in your account.

Cooks

How do cooks join and receive requests?+

Cooks create a profile and complete the current review process. Eligible requests appear in the cook's Requests area, where they can accept or decline.

Where can cooks see their earnings?+

The cook dashboard includes earnings and cash-out history.

Payments & plans

What does a household pay for?+

Review the plan and service price shown in the current booking flow. CookCircle's existing plan and payment rules remain in effect.

Does a one-time booking charge a real card?+

The current one-time booking payment step is simulated and does not charge a real card.

Schedules & leave

How can a cook update availability?+

Cooks can update availability by date and session in their dashboard. For time off affecting an assigned booking, contact support so the current booking process can be followed.

What if a cook becomes unavailable?+

The booking follows CookCircle's existing backup-required and, where applicable, service-credit process. A replacement cook is not guaranteed.

Verification & trust

How are cooks reviewed?+

Cook profiles and supporting information go through the platform's existing review and approval process, including service-society approval.

No matching questions. Try another search or contact support.

FOR COOKS

Practical questions.

How do I become a CookCircle cook?+

Start with the existing Become a Cook signup. The platform reviews cook profiles and supporting information before approval.

What information do I need to provide?+

The signup flow asks for a cook profile and service-society information. Follow the fields shown in the current application.

How do households get matched with me?+

Requests are sent to active, approved cooks whose profile serves that society and who are available for the session.

How does scheduling work?+

Accepted bookings appear in your schedule with the assigned date, session and household details.

How do I request leave?+

You can update availability by date and session. If time off affects an assigned booking, contact support; the current flow does not guarantee an automatic replacement.

Where can I see my earnings?+

Your cook dashboard includes earnings and cash-out history.
